# About WeSphere

## 🌟 What is WeSphere?

WeSphere is a unique and innovative character discovery and claiming app that brings together students, friends, and classmates in a fun, interactive way. The app allows users to explore, claim, and manage virtual character personalities based on traits and information collected from different students in their class and friend circles.

## 🎯 Purpose & Vision

### The Main Purpose
WeSphere was created to bridge the gap between digital entertainment and real-world social connections. The app serves as a platform where:

- **Students can discover character representations** of themselves and their classmates
- **Friends can explore personality traits** collected from their social circles
- **Users can claim and personalize characters** that resonate with them
- **Communities can engage** through character sharing and discovery

### Why It Was Made
The app was developed to:

1. **Foster Social Connections**: Create a fun way for students and friends to interact and learn about each other through character representations
2. **Entertainment Value**: Provide an engaging platform for character discovery and collection
3. **Educational Aspect**: Help users understand personality traits and characteristics in a gamified way
4. **Community Building**: Bring people together through shared character experiences

## 🚀 How It Works

### Core Functionality

#### 1. **Character Discovery**
- Browse through a collection of virtual character personalities
- Search for specific characters by name or traits
- Explore character details including personality, nature, and speech patterns

#### 2. **Character Claiming System**
- Users can request to claim characters that represent them
- Admin approval system ensures fair distribution
- Claimed characters appear in the user's "My Card" section

#### 3. **Character Creation & Management**
- Users can request new characters to be added
- Detailed character profiles with traits and information
- Customizable character cards and representations

#### 4. **Social Features**
- Invite friends to join the platform
- Share character collections
- Explore characters created by others

### User Experience Flow

1. **Registration**: Users sign up with email and verify through OTP
2. **Onboarding**: Interactive tutorial explaining the app features
3. **Character Exploration**: Browse and search through available characters
4. **Character Claiming**: Request to claim characters that represent them
5. **Profile Management**: Customize their profile and manage claimed characters
6. **Social Interaction**: Invite friends and share character discoveries
